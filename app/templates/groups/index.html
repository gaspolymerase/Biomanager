How groups work

  • Wherever you choose Shared or Lab common, you can pick one of your groups instead: a cage, a breeding tank, a stock vial, a plasmid or a reagent shared with a group is its members’ to edit. Everyone in the lab still sees it, as now.
  • A database made for a group (Who is it for? when you add one) is seen by its members only, and admins.
  • On the calendar, a to-do can be yours, a group’s or the lab’s. A group’s members see it and can tick it off.
  • A notebook page or template can be shared with a group, to view or to edit.
  • The colony’s My groups view shows the mice and cages of everyone in your groups.
