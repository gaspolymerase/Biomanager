## BioManager 1.0.2

### New

- **Sheets you read at a glance**, in every database:
  - **The page scrolls, not the sheet.** The search, chips and buttons stay at the top of the window, with the column names under them. The count and pages stay at the bottom. **All** per page lists every row on one page.
  - Text is smaller and columns are tighter, so more fits across without scrolling sideways.
- **New, at the bottom left**, adds an empty row at the end of the sheet, with its first cell ready to type in.
  - A strain, fish, fish line, plasmid or order needs a name or a tank first, so there the button opens its form.
- **The dot shows an animal's age**:
  - **blue** for young, **green** for adult, **red** for old;
  - mice: under 8 weeks, 8 to 30 weeks, past 30 weeks;
  - fish: under 3 months, up to 18 months, past 18 months;
  - vials and plates: progeny still developing, adults, past the flip or chunk;
  - grey once an animal is not alive. Point at a dot to see what its colour means.
- **Expired reagents, antibodies and viruses are red**: the dot, the number and the name, as well as the date.
- **Cages**:
  - *Litter born* and *Wean due* come right after the mice, so when to wean is always in view.
  - Type over a cage's number to renumber it. Its mice go with it, and a number another cage has is refused.
- **Sequence**, beside each plasmid's number, opens its sequence and map. A plasmid with none yet shows **Add sequence**.

### What works differently

- **New litters are numbered L-1, L-2, L-3…**, one past the highest L-number the lab has. The litter sheet sorts them by number, so L-2 comes before L-10. Litters you named another way keep their names.
- **Every database has a name of its own.** A name another database has, in any case and including Mouse colony, Zebrafish and Plasmids, is refused when you create or rename one. This way `@` and a database's name in a notebook page always mean one database. Databases that already share a name keep it until one is renamed.
- **Table, Boxes and List** for plasmids, and **Table, Rack grid and Genotyping queue** for fish tanks, now sit above the sheet, where every database has them.
- A cage's number is now typed in rather than clicked. Its arrow, or its mice count, opens the cage.
- Hidden columns and widths you set on the Cages tab are reset once, because its columns moved.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.0.2, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app.
- **Lab server**:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.

  There is no database change this time. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).
