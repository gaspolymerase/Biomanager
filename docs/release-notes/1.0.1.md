## BioManager 1.0.1

### New

- **An Experiments tab in every animal database**: Zebrafish, fly and worm
  stocks and the lab's organism databases now have *Experiments* beside
  their tanks, fish and clutches (or stocks, or records), working as the
  mouse colony's does: put animals under a shared treatment plan, record
  what is done each day and follow a readout suited to the animal (or your
  own). A new experiment can start with all the animals of a tank, rack or
  housing.
- **Cages show their mice again**: on the Cages tab every cage opens with
  its mice beneath it, ready for weaning, moving and the rest. **Close all**
  / **Open all** folds them and is remembered; the arrow, the cage's number
  or its mice count opens one. **Cards** shows each cage as its own card.

### What works differently

- **Only Breeder cages are shared with the lab.** A Breeder cage starts
  shared, and its owner (or an admin) can make it personal. Other cages
  are their owner's: on updating, a Breeding or Stock cage that was marked
  shared becomes personal, and Breeder cages that were shared stay so.
  Admins still see every cage.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.0.1, or download
  it below. Your data is kept outside the app and a copy is taken before
  the database is upgraded.
- **Lab server**: take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`),
  unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder (your `.env`,
  backups and certificates are kept), then `host/load-image.sh` and
  `docker compose up -d --build`. The database upgrades itself on start;
  every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing
  lost. Details: *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in
  the bundle).
