## BioManager 1.0.3

### New

- **Project groups** (*More → Project groups*): some of the lab working on one project.
  - An admin makes a group and picks its members. A group's leads can add and take out members. Someone can be in several groups.
  - Wherever you could share something with the lab, you can share it with one of your groups instead. A cage, plasmid, reagent, breeding tank or lab stock vial shared with a group is its members' to edit. Everyone in the lab still sees it.
  - A database made for a group (**Who is it for?** when you add one) is seen only by its members and admins. **Share with the group** on the Databases page gives one of your own databases to a group.
  - A notebook page can be shared with a group, to view or to edit, and a template can be for a group.
  - **My groups**, beside My colony, shows the mice and cages of everyone in your groups.
  - Deleting a group makes what was shared with it its owner's own again.
- **Who sees it**, on calendar events and to-dos: **Only me**, **Everyone in the lab**, or one of your groups.
  - Your own events and to-dos are seen by nobody else, admins included.
  - A shared event can be changed only by the person who added it and admins.
  - A shared to-do can be ticked off by anyone it is for.
  - A new event starts as the lab's, a new to-do as yours.
- **Any cage can be shared**, by its owner or an admin, with the lab or a group.
  - A **Breeder** cage starts shared. Every other cage starts personal, and a cage that stops being a breeder cage goes back to personal.
  - The Cages tab has a chip for each purpose (*Experiments*, *Breeder*…) to show only those cages.
- **Settings → Devices** shows which device holds the lab's master copy, and every computer linked to the lab.
  - A desktop app linked to the lab's server can **Open the lab in this window**, to work on the shared lab in the app.
  - A desktop can **Share this lab on the network**, for the other computers on the same network.
  - An admin can **Make it the master**, handing the master copy to a linked desktop. The other device turns read only and points everyone there. **Give the master copy back** returns it.
  - Nothing is ever merged: there is one master copy at a time.

### What works differently

- **Other people's to-dos are no longer on your calendar.** Each person's own to-dos are theirs. Use a lab or group to-do for work that others should see.
- **Only its owner, or an admin, changes or deletes a calendar event.** Before, anyone could.
- **Away soon** lists only someone's shared to-dos, not their personal ones.
- A sample whose source is a mouse number too big for the database is now saved with the usual "no mouse" warning. Before, on a lab server, the sample was refused and the samples sheet could stop opening.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.0.3, or download it below. Your data is kept outside the app, and a copy is taken before the database is upgraded.
- **Lab server**:
  1. Take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`).
  2. Unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder. Your `.env`, backups and certificates are kept.
  3. Run `host/load-image.sh`, then `docker compose up -d --build`.

  The database upgrades itself on start; every earlier release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Every event the lab already has stays shared with the lab, and existing to-dos become their owners'. Details are under *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle). If the app ever says it is read only, see *The app says it is read only* there.
