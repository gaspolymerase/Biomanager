What it does for you

{{ icon('mouse') }}

Colonies and stocks

Mice, zebrafish, flies, worms or any organism: who is in which cage, which litter needs weaning, which vials need flipping, which breeders are getting old.

{{ icon('qrcode') }}

At the rack

Cage cards with a QR code. Scan one with your phone and that cage opens, ready to edit, without walking back to a computer.

{{ icon('plasmid') }}

Plasmids and supplies

Plasmid maps, samples in freezer boxes, orders from request to shelf, and reagents that warn you before they expire.

{{ icon('calendar-clock') }}

A calendar that fills itself

Weanings, genotyping, flips and expiry dates appear on their own, beside equipment bookings, protocol timelines and who's away.

{{ icon('notebook') }}

A notebook you write together

Experiments, protocols to run at the bench, data sheets with plots and statistics, all linked to the animals they describe.

{{ icon('users') }}

Made for a whole lab

You edit your own records and see everyone's. Every change is kept with who made it, and bulk changes can be undone.
