You'll be told when it matters

The {{ icon('bell') }} at the top shows what happened while you were away:

  • Transfers: someone moved your animals or vials, gave you some, or put an animal in your cage or tank.
  • Genotyping: a genotype was recorded for your animals, or they were marked for genotyping. Once a day you also see what of yours is still waiting.
  • Orders: an order you placed was ordered, arrived or was cancelled.
  • Lab news: a database was added for the lab.

Turn any of them off in Settings.
