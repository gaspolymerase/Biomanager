## BioManager 1.0

The first version we call finished: tested for weeks as whole labs would use
it, by a mouse lab, a fish, fly and worm lab and a wet lab doing cloning,
cell culture, protein and RNA work, and by testers trying to break it. How
it was tested, and what they found and what was fixed, is in
[How BioManager 1.0 was tested](https://github.com/gaspolymerase/biomanager/blob/master/docs/TESTING.md).

### New

- **Utilities: 41 bench calculators**, answering as you type: molarity,
  dilutions and serial dilutions, percent and ×-fold stocks, buffer pH,
  osmolarity; A₂₆₀, DNA moles and copies, oligo Tm, ligation and Gibson,
  PCR and qPCR master mixes, qPCR efficiency, ΔΔCt; A₂₈₀, protein MW, ε and
  pI from the sequence, BCA and Bradford standard curves, SDS-PAGE recipes;
  cell counts, seeding, doubling time, transfection, MOI, lentivirus titer,
  freezing down; OD₆₀₀, antibiotics, rpm ↔ × g, doses by body weight,
  agarose gels, radioactive decay, statistics and units. Reference tables
  for vessels, buffers, antibiotics, gels, isotopes and molecular weights.
- **Customize Home**: choose which cards Home shows, their order and width,
  and add your to-dos, instrument bookings, recent pages and calculators.
- **Primers & oligos** and **Cell lines** databases, ready-made.
- **@links to any record** in a notebook page (`@antibodies 5`), found by
  name, catalogue number or lot; the chip shows the record's name, and the
  record lists the pages that link it (*Used in notebook pages*).
- **Numbers for results**: concentration and purity on samples and plasmid
  tubes, pasted straight from a Nanodrop column into the sheet.
- **Positions that look after themselves**: Add many fills a box side by
  side, a used-up tube frees its place, moving a box updates *Stored at*.
- **Repeating and duplicated bookings**, instruments you can rename, and
  calendar repeats such as *the first Monday of the month*.
- Templates that keep a page's type and can keep only its structure;
  labels where you choose what each says; A₂₈₀ in the calculator block;
  *Order again* that says when something is already on order.

### What works differently

- **The sidebar's foot is shorter**: **More** holds Batch history and the
  admin pages (Lab setup, Colony overview, Audit log, Manage users, Guests,
  Racks & boxes); **Help** holds the user guide and Send feedback.
- **A plasmid's page is at its number**: plasmid #12 is `…/plasmid/12`. Old
  links (`…/plasmids/19`) still open the right plasmid.
- **Renaming a database changes its address** to the new name. The old
  address keeps working, so printed labels, bookmarks and `@mentions` in
  notebook pages still find it.
- A page started with a topic open goes in that topic, and in *Inbox*
  when none is; search puts names that start with what you typed first,
  and a lot or catalogue number typed in full before them; times on
  notebook pages are on the lab's clock.
- A sheet keeps your sort after a reload, and its chip is in the address
  (`?chip=…`), so a bookmark opens it the same way.

### Updating

- **Desktop app**: *Install and Restart* when it offers 1.0.0, or download
  it below. Your data is kept outside the app and a copy is taken before
  the database is upgraded.
- **Lab server**: take a fresh backup (`docker compose exec backup backup.sh`),
  unpack the new `biomanager-server.tar.gz` over the old folder (your `.env`,
  backups and certificates are kept), then `host/load-image.sh` and
  `docker compose up -d --build`. The database upgrades itself on start
  (the log says `Upgrading the database from … to …`); every earlier
  release's database was checked to upgrade with nothing lost. Details:
  *Updating the app* in the runbook (`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` in the bundle).

### Known limits

Things the labs asked for that come after 1.0: linking a sample to what is
made from it (RNA → cDNA → plate), @links to fly vials and organisms, a cell-culture database with Passage
actions, booking a chain of instruments at once, freezer and equipment
logs, and a qPCR plate-layout block.
